Requirement SYM (user, 2026-10-30, non-negotiable). The ground state, and every state of the thermal manifold, must have all the symmetries of the Hamiltonian, even at the price of a less converged energy. Every selection, cap, memory-guard, trim or amplitude cut acts on whole symmetry orbits (S± partners, symmetry images). When the budget cannot hold the symmetric basis, every member loses accuracy equally; a partial orbit is never kept. Fixes that trade symmetry for energy are rejected.

Evidence lives in debug/gs_review_2026-10/ (gitignored): - findings.md, the running log; - reviews_round1.md, the condensed rev. 2 reviews; - the harness scripts and logs/; - the reviewers’ probes in review_{physics,numerics,mpi,code}/.

Baseline: the GS test subset passes at 1, 2 and 3 ranks (508, 515 and 515 passed; logs/baseline_*). None of the defects below is caught by the current tests.

8. Decisions

  • O1. mixed_valence default for RSPt/Σ/DC: auto (user, 2026-10-30). The J3 rule applies: auto never raises.
  • O2. DC-only tolerance overrides: keep them (user, 2026-10-30).
    • Why they exist: a rough DC estimate, for cases where the DC search would otherwise burn enormous compute just finding the lowest sector.
    • When they can go: once no reasonably simple calculation can spend unreasonable time on that. Today one can: FCC Ni’s walk did not finish in 90 min, and the losing N2 sectors take 513–954 s. Revisit after S1, B1, B5 and B7 are measured.
    • DC_E_PT2_TOL/DC_DE2_MIN default to unset, which means the GS values (config.py:897-973). So they are opt-in, and DC→Σ reuse (B8) applies whenever they are unset.
    • When the user loosens them, reuse only the DC’s sector choice: start the Σ walk at the DC winner, still checking its ±1 neighbours. Do not reuse its states.
    • The three DC tolerance rules (P5) still collapse to one precedence: DC knob if set, else the spec.
  • O3. Thermal re-solve below the walk’s quality: how it happens, and the rule (user: SYM, non-negotiable).

    • Mechanism. The walk’s Lowest solve converges one state of the winning sector on its own basis: SMO 3-bath 247,974 determinants. The thermal Within solve rebuilds from the seed and must hold the whole window in one basis. On SMO that is a 4-fold S=3/2 level whose S_z members live on disjoint determinant sets. The auto cap is sized “for the ground state alone”, so each member gets about a quarter of the walk’s room:

      run thermal basis per member walk’s single state
      SMO 3-bath -n 4 452,240 ~113k 248k
      SMO 3-bath -n 1 509,546 ~127k 248k
      SMO 4-bath cap 440,512 ~110k the full 440,512

    • The energy deficit is acceptable; the symmetry breaking is not. A less converged but symmetric thermal manifold is the correct outcome under SYM. The defect is S2: once the cap binds, the expansion grows and cuts members unevenly, and the quartet splits.

    • Resolution:

        1. S2: symmetric (orbit-closed) growth and truncation in the Within expand, so a binding cap costs every member the same accuracy;
        1. resolve the cap for the Within manifold (the winning level’s multiplicity × the walk’s per-state basis, bounded by memory) together with P10, so the symmetric solve is as accurate as memory allows;
        1. no re-solve and no “never above the walk” floor: seeding from the walk’s single state would itself break the symmetry.

      A degenerate-level split beyond roundoff is reported as an error-level warning, because under SYM it can only mean a bug.

  • O4. χ and spectra read mixed_valence from the archive (user, 2026-10-30).
    • When the archive has it, use it. When it is missing, use the χ or spectra default (off).
    • The archive records the setting (auto/true/false), not the resolved membership, so a replay re-decides membership on its own model.
    • Archives written before MV hold None or 0, which map to the reading driver’s default as MV already specifies.
  • O5. Freeze only on explicit user request (user, 2026-10-30), e.g. a shell declared role = "core". No automatic detection.
    • Where the user freezes a shell, check ‖[H, N_core]‖ once and warn with its size if it is not zero. The seed-only pin then leaks, and the user should know.
    • MS F1 freezes a shell only if the user declares it core, never on its own.
  • O6. Parameters still to choose: B1’s margin; the J3 weight-error threshold; MV decision K (KMS); the J1/O5 commutator warning tolerance; the CI runtime budget of the promoted oracle.
